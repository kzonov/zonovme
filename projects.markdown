A collection of side projects, apps, and things I've built outside of work.

### [TriangleCore.rocks](https://trianglecore.rocks/)

A small shop I run with my life partner, dedicated to electronic music hardware — Eurorack modules, standalone synthesizers, and merch from independent hardware makers. We help musicians explore unique synths and give indie builders a platform to reach a wider audience.

### [Flora Synths](https://trianglecore.rocks/) ([Instagram](https://instagram.com/flora.synths))

Our own line of Eurorack modules and accessories, sold through TriangleCore. Includes modules like the *Quatrovertalius* (four channels of chained attenuversion) and custom blank panels in multiple colors.

### [Chord to Music](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chord-to-music/id6752472491) (iOS)

A chord progression generator for musicians. Creates authentic progressions across 13+ genres and visualizes them as piano roll, guitar tab, or chord notes — a quick way to spark musical ideas.

### [54321 Breath – Grounding App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/54321-breath-grounding-app/id6758555110) (iOS)

A mindfulness app that guides you through the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique to help manage anxiety and stress by reconnecting with your senses.

### [DiskViz](https://github.com/kzonov/disk-viz)

An open-source tool for visualizing disk usage — helps you see where your storage is actually going. Written for my own curiosity and shared on GitHub.

### [Wording Taboo Game](/taboo.html)

A simple German-language Tabu/Guess-It game I built for family game nights. Shows German words (with articles and translations) at different difficulty levels for vocabulary practice.
